autom@tedVisualMusiC (2001) is a generative audiovisual net-art work by Sergio Maltagliati, part of the VisualMusic series exploring browser-based real-time transformation of visual and sound elements. The work belongs to a research lineage continuing Pietro Grossi's HomeArt experiments and network-based interactive composition.

Originally implemented in Flash/SWF, the work became inaccessible following the end of Flash support in mainstream browsers. The artist has initiated migration toward HTML5 to preserve the generative logic, visual behavior, and sound interaction structure.
