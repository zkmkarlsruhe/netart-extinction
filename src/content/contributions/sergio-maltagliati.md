Italian artist and researcher working in generative audiovisual systems, net art, and interactive media since the 1990s, in continuity with the experimental lineage of Pietro Grossi. Sent preliminary contribution materials in May 2026 covering historical context for netOper@ and the VisualMusic series, the Flash/SWF to HTML5 migration effort, the VisualMusic YouTube channel removal, and the artist's ongoing Internet Archive preservation profile. Followed up with a bilingual dossier on neXtOper@ / Oper@_PiXeL (2001–2003), an early mobile-media reinterpretation of opera exhibited at Galerie De Meerse (Amsterdam) and Palazzo Vecchio (Florence).
